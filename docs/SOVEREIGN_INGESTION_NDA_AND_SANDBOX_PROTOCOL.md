### ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS & AXIOMATIC CLASSIFICATION

**1.1 "Proprietary Ingestion Data"** means all technical specifications, mathematical parameters, failure logs, chemical/molecular structures, CAD bounds, finite element meshes, sensor telemetry, source code, and empirical documentation disclosed by the Enterprise Partner to Sentinari for the purpose of the 4-Hour Challenge.

**1.2 "Axiomatic Invariants"** means universal, non-proprietary principles of mathematics, physics, thermodynamics, information theory, and statistics that govern physical reality (e.g., Navier-Stokes equations, Shannon entropy, sorted array invariants, Gaussian distributions). Axiomatic Invariants are not proprietary to either Party.

**1.3 "Sovereign Air-Gapped Environment"** means a dedicated, physically isolated computing infrastructure operated locally by Sentinari, possessing zero outbound network sockets to public large language model (LLM) APIs, zero third-party telemetry monitors, and full cryptographic memory sandboxing.

**1.4 "Validation Deliverable"** means the formal 15-to-18-page publication-grade R&D Validation Paper, KaTeX mathematical proofs, and executable simulation prototype models (Python/MATLAB) generated by Sentinari during the 4-hour audit.

### ARTICLE 2: THE SOVEREIGN ZERO-CLOUD TRANSMISSION COVENANT

**2.1 Strict Zero-Cloud Policy:** Sentinari contractually covenants and warrants that Proprietary Ingestion Data:
* Shall **never** be transmitted, routed, mirrored, or cached across any commercial public cloud API (including, but not limited to, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Cloud Vertex public endpoints, Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services, or Amazon Web Services).
* Shall be computed exclusively within a local, air-gapped environment utilizing locally instantiated models (e.g., offline Gemma models, local Pseudo-Dynamics tensor engines).

**2.2 Cryptographic Isolation & Memory Sandboxing:**
* Upon receipt, all submitted data is ingested through the *Gateway Guardian* filter, assigned a unique SHA-256 cryptographic digest, and placed into an isolated runtime workspace.
* Outbound networking is strictly restricted to ensure zero telemetry leakage during the entirety of the 4-hour adversarial processing cycle.

---

### ARTICLE 3: STRICT NON-TRAINING & MODEL RETENTION BAN

**3.1 Zero Foundation Model Training:** Sentinari unequivocally warrants that no portion of the Enterprise Partner's Proprietary Ingestion Data, whether raw, preprocessed, or synthesized, shall ever be used to:
* Pre-train, fine-tune, or adjust weights of any foundation model, neural network, or algorithmic database accessible to third parties or future clients.
* Enhance public open-source or proprietary models.

**3.2 Ephemeral Processing & Cryptographic Purge:**
* The Enterprise Partner's raw input files exist in volatile local storage solely for the duration required to complete the 4-hour audit and compile the Validation Deliverable.
* Within seventy-two (72) hours following delivery of the validation paper, Sentinari will permanently wipe and cryptographically purge all raw working telemetry from local disk storage, retaining only the delivered validation report for archive verification, unless explicitly requested otherwise by the Enterprise Partner.
* A signed **Cryptographic Certificate of Destruction** verifying the SHA-256 wipe will be provided upon written request.

### ARTICLE 5: PERMITTED ANONYMIZATION PROTOCOL

**5.1 Enterprise Data Sanitization Right:**
The Enterprise Partner is explicitly encouraged and permitted to sanitize, abstract, or anonymize its Proprietary Ingestion Data prior to transmission. Sentinari’s adversarial pattern recognition engine operates on pure physical, numerical, and structural relationships; therefore:
* Commercial product brand names may be replaced with arbitrary identifiers (e.g., `Component_Alpha`).
* Absolute operational magnitudes may be normalized against non-dimensional ratios (e.g., $x / x_{max}$).
* Exact proprietary chemical designations may be abstracted to functional classifications and stoichiometric constraints.

**5.2 Sufficient Technical Resolution:**
The Enterprise Partner agrees to preserve sufficient mathematical, dimensional, and thermodynamic fidelity to permit rigorous axiomatic stress-testing.
