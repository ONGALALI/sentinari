🎥 How to Record a Video Tour with Auto-Pause Drawing & Yellow Checkmarks

  1. Open the Video Studio by clicking 🎥 Record Video Tour at the top right of the Web Landing Page or navigating to /ui/video.
  2. Select a workspace pill (e.g., Playground, Pattern Discovery, or Qualia Lab) in the top selector. Gemma will autonomously write an authentic, custom narration script based on live workspace telemetry!
  3. Click the 🖥️ Live Playground UI toolbar button to display the authentic 21-discipline Playground workspace on the 1080p canvas.
  4. To annotate or highlight on screen:
    • Click 🔲 Box to draw bounding boxes around key elements.
    • Click ↗ Line to draw directional indicator arrows.
    • Click ✔ Yellow Tick to mark verified concepts.
    • Click ✏️ Pen for freehand diagramming.
    Notice: Clicking any drawing tool automatically pauses playback so you can calmly annotate without rushing!
  5. During playback, the camera smoothly zooms in on the topic under discussion for a brief focal moment, terminating in an animated bright yellow checkmark (✔ VERIFIED TOPIC).
  6. Click ⚡ Render Broadcast 1080p MP4 to produce a complete broadcast master with synchronized voiceover, subtitle tracks (.SRT), and ambient soundscape.
