#### 1. Chou-Talalay Combination Index Equation:
$$\mathbf{CI = \frac{D_{\text{Statin}}}{(D_x)_{\text{Statin}}} + \frac{D_{\text{Cytotoxic}}}{(D_x)_{\text{Cytotoxic}}} + \alpha \frac{D_{\text{Statin}} \cdot D_{\text{Cytotoxic}}}{(D_x)_{\text{Statin}} \cdot (D_x)_{\text{Cytotoxic}}}}$$
* Where $D_1, D_2$ are the combination doses yielding effect $x$, and $(D_x)_1, (D_x)_2$ are the iso-effective single-agent doses.
