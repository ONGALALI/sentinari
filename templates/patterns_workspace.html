Containers below are named for their scientific fields. Cross-field patterns appear in combination containers (Field A + Field B). Every container shows live recognition progress, the potential benefits of each pattern, and an APA 7th edition report (HTML preview / PDF download) covering all fields in the container, its data sources, and its Works Cited.
The scoring law behind this workspace: impact dimensionsiImpact dimensions (7). Knowledge, Intelligence, Decision, Human Agency, Societal, Scientific, Risk. Each level x_i (0–10) is measured from the pattern's real extraction record: evidence-chunk depth, source-document breadth, entity/relation graph mass, pipeline confidence, cross-field span, and recorded benefits. Sustainability Weights: Knowledge 0.18 · Human Agency 0.16 · Societal 0.16 · Intelligence 0.14 · Decision 0.14 · Scientific 0.14 · Risk 0.08., level bandsiImpact level bands. Normalized Dimension Impact SD_i(%) = (W_i × S_i(x)) ÷ NSIS × 100, where NSIS = Σ W_j·S_j(x) is the Net Sustainable Impact Score. Bands: 0–33.33% Level 1 (Low) · 33.34–67.67% Level 2 (Medium) · 67.68–100% Level 3 (High)., and the coupled bell curveiCoupled bell curve. Each dimension has a sustainability function S_i(x) = G_i(x) ÷ A_i with G_i(x) = A_i·exp(−(x−μ_i)² ÷ (2σ_i²)). Coupling rules from the Assessment Matrix: Intelligence > 7 contracts Human Agency (μ −0.4·excess, σ −0.15·excess); Risk > 5 widens Societal σ (+0.3·excess) and drops Decision μ (−0.25·excess); Scientific > 7 lifts Knowledge amplitude (+0.05, capped 1.0)..
